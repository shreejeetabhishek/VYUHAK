# VYUHAK v26 INFINITY // Continuous Multi-Scale Earth, Solar System, Galaxy & Universe

VYUHAK (*Virtual Yield Uplink & Heuristic Aerospace Kinematics*) is a serverless, mobile-first 3D/2D orbital intelligence and continuous cosmic-scale visualization platform powered by Three.js custom GLSL shaders, Leaflet.js, and 100% free, keyless live APIs.

## What's New in v26 INFINITY
- **Continuous Pinch/Scroll Auto-Scale Zoom:** Pinching or scrolling past the boundary of any scale smoothly warps across **Earth** -> **Heliocentric Solar System** -> **Volumetric Milky Way Galaxy** -> **3D Cosmic Web, Observable Universe (CMB Horizon) & Hypothetical Multiverse**.
- **Live Logarithmic Scale Bar:** Real-time distance ruler in the bottom-left corner updating every frame from kilometers (`km`) to Astronomical Units (`AU`) to thousands and billions of light-years (`ly`).
- **Persistent "YOU ARE HERE" 3D-to-2D Callout:** Tracks Earth, the Solar System, and the Laniakea Supercluster across all cosmic scales.
- **Observable Universe & Multiverse Mode (`Universe`):** Renders 3D supercluster filaments, a Planck-inspired **Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB)** horizon sphere (`93B ly`), surrounding hypothetical multiverse bubbles, and a live **Universe Data** dossier in the bottom drawer.
