# VYUHAK // 3D Earth Observation, Solar System, Galaxy & Multiverse Atlas

VYUHAK (*Virtual Yield Uplink & Heuristic Aerospace Kinematics*) is a zero-backend, mobile-first 3D/2D Earth Observation, Heliocentric Solar System, Volumetric Milky Way Galaxy, Observable Universe, and Multiverse visualization platform.

## Core Capabilities
- **3D & 2D Earth Observation:** Real-time solar terminator and city lights, 3D atmospheric scattering and cloud cover, live International Space Station (ISS) tracking, custom orbital propagation (`VK-Tracker-01`), RainViewer weather radar, USGS earthquakes, NASA EONET events, and sub-satellite ground dossiers.
- **Heliocentric Keplerian Solar System:** Live NASA JPL J2000 orbital ephemerides for all 8 planets, major moons, dwarf planets & asteroids (Ceres, Vesta, Pallas, Pluto, Haumea, Makemake, Eris, Sedna), Asteroid/Kuiper/Oort belts, Comet 1P/Halley with dual ion and dust tails, Voyager 1, internal layer structure, bulk density, magnetosphere, Earth Similarity Index (ESI), and habitability profiles.
- **Milky Way Galaxy, Exoplanets & Local Group:** Volumetric barred spiral disk with dust lanes, 35,000 multi-scale stars, Sagittarius A*, nebulae, confirmed Earth-like habitable-zone exoplanets (Proxima Centauri b, TRAPPIST-1e, TOI-700 d, LHS 1140 b, Kepler-442b, Kepler-186f), and nearby galaxies (Andromeda M31, Triangulum M33, LMC, SMC, Centaurus A, Sombrero M104).
- **Observable Universe & Multiverse:** 3D Cosmic Web supercluster filaments and voids (Laniakea, Virgo, Great Attractor, Coma Cluster, Shapley, Sloan Great Wall, Hercules–Corona Borealis Great Wall, Boötes Void, GN-z11), CMB horizon shell, and surrounding theoretical pocket universes with node-anchored multi-scale zoom.

Upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`, and `README.md` directly to your static web host or GitHub Pages repository.
