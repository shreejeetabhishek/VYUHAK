# VYUHAK v43 DEEP-ZOOM // Ultra-Deep Zoom-Out & Luminous Pocket Universes

VYUHAK (*Virtual Yield Uplink & Heuristic Aerospace Kinematics*) is a serverless, mobile-first 3D/2D Earth Observation, Heliocentric Solar System, Volumetric Milky Way Galaxy, Observable Universe, and Multiverse platform.

## What's Upgraded in v43 DEEP-ZOOM
- **Ultra-Deep Zoom-Out Across Every Stage:**
  - Expanded camera far plane (`3600` units) and raised `controls.maxDistance` and pinch-out thresholds across every stage (`48` in Earth, `320` in Solar System, `320` in Galaxy, `185` in Universe, and `950` in Multiverse) with a panoramic `(0, 210, 380)` Multiverse default view.
- **Clearer Neighboring Pocket Universes (While Keeping Our Universe Primary):**
  - Added translucent horizon shells, brighter internal cosmic-web galaxy nodes, and filament strands inside every surrounding pocket universe so they read clearly against deep space while remaining visually softer than **Our Universe** at the center.
