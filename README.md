# VYUHAK v34 PHOTOREAL // Cinema-Grade 1P/Halley Comet & Futuristic Holographic HUD

VYUHAK (*Virtual Yield Uplink & Heuristic Aerospace Kinematics*) is a serverless, mobile-first 3D/2D Earth Observation, Heliocentric Solar System, Volumetric Milky Way Galaxy, Observable Universe, and Multiverse platform.

## What's Upgraded in v34 PHOTOREAL
- **Cinema-Grade Comet 1P/Halley:**
  - **Giotto/Rosetta Bilobed Nucleus Shader:** Irregular 15×8 km peanut-shaped carbonaceous rock nucleus with procedural FBM ridges, craters, dark charcoal albedo, and glowing sunlit icy sublimation fissures.
  - **Sun-Side Outgassing Jet & 3-Layer Coma:** Active sunlit sublimation plume erupting toward the Sun, surrounded by a sharp white core, dusty inner halo, and diffuse cyan hydrogen envelope.
  - **3-Axis Volumetric Dual Tails + Live Flowing Particles:** Crossed 0°/60°/120° animated GLSL plasma & dust ribbons (zero hard edges from any 3D camera angle) paired with 460 live-streaming ion and dust particles drifting dynamically away from the coma.
  - **Live NASA JPL J2000 Ephemeris:** Exact retrograde orbit (`I = 162.26°`, `e = 0.96714`, `P = 75.32 yrs`) with `1 Yr/s` and `5 Yrs/s` Time Warp modes.
- **Automatic Cache-Busting (`?v=34`):** Ensures mobile browsers immediately load the new photorealistic Halley's Comet and ultra-transparent holographic HUD labels without showing stale cached files.
