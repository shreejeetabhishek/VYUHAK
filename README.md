# VYUHAK // 3D Earth Observation, Solar System, Galaxy & Multiverse Atlas (v2.0 Final)

VYUHAK (*Virtual Yield Uplink & Heuristic Aerospace Kinematics*) is a zero-backend, mobile-first 3D/2D Earth Observation, Heliocentric Solar System, Volumetric Milky Way Galaxy, Observable Universe, and Multiverse scientific atlas.

## Core Capabilities
- **3D & 2D Earth Observation:** Real-time solar terminator and city lights, 3D atmospheric scattering and clouds, live International Space Station (ISS) tracking, 90-minute orbital ground-track projection, LEO/SSO/GEO orbit presets, orbital period/horizon calculator, RainViewer weather radar, USGS earthquakes, NASA EONET events, and sub-satellite ground dossiers.
- **Heliocentric Keplerian Solar System:** Live NASA JPL J2000 orbital ephemerides for all 8 planets, major moons, dwarf planets & asteroids (Ceres, Vesta, Pallas, Pluto, Haumea, Makemake, Eris, Sedna), 3D Planetary Core Cutaway Slice mode, Sun–Earth L1–L5 Lagrange points & Snow Line, deep-space probes (Voyager 1, Voyager 2, New Horizons, Parker Solar Probe), Comet 1P/Halley with dual ion and dust tails, Earth Similarity Index (ESI), comparative world physics vs. Earth, and NOAA SWPC live space weather.
- **Milky Way Galaxy, Exoplanet Mini-Orreries & Local Group:** Volumetric barred spiral disk with dust lanes, 35,000 multi-scale stars, relativistic accretion disk on Sagittarius A*, NFW Dark Matter Halo overlay, interactive 3D Exoplanet Star-System Mini-Orreries (TRAPPIST-1, Proxima Centauri, TOI-700, Kepler-442, LHS 1140, Kepler-186), and nearby galaxies (Andromeda M31, Triangulum M33, LMC, SMC, Centaurus A, Sombrero M104).
- **Observable Universe & Multiverse:** 3D Cosmic Web supercluster filaments and voids (Laniakea, Virgo, Great Attractor, Coma Cluster, Shapley, Sloan Great Wall, Hercules–Corona Borealis Great Wall, Boötes Void, GN-z11), interactive Cosmic Redshift ($z$) & Lookback-Time scrubber, CMB horizon shell, and surrounding theoretical pocket universes with node-anchored multi-scale zoom.
